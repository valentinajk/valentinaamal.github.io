      Financial Filings Analyst Agent

      Project, analytics and BI

      UK Mortgage Market MI Dashboard

      A reporting pipeline on real Bank of England mortgage lending data, 2020 to 2025. Python cleans the data, five SQL queries (window functions, CTEs, LAG and RANK) analyse it, and a three-page Power BI dashboard tracks lending, arrears risk and momentum.

      Lending fell 33% in 2020-Q2 and peaked at £89.1bn in 2021-Q2. Arrears moved into high-risk territory in 2023-Q4 as interest rates rose.

      Project, analytics and ML

      Indian Job Market Intelligence

      An analysis of more than 97,000 raw job postings, with cleaning, outlier removal, and a Streamlit dashboard with filters by city and role type. A salary prediction tool sits on Linear Regression and Random Forest models. Both explain little of the variation in salary (R² of 0.17 and 0.07), and the project reports that openly.

      After cleaning, 29,000+ postings remain. Advertised salaries climb steeply with experience, from about ₹2.6 lakh a year at 0 to 2 years to ₹10 lakh at 10+ years.

      Project, forecasting

      UK Tourism Forecasting

      A Prophet time-series model on World Bank data for international arrivals to the UK. The notebook reshapes the data, trains the model, and plots the forecast and its trend.

      Arrivals reached about 37.7 million in 2017. The baseline forecast is about 42.6 million by the end of 2027, within a range of 40.2 to 45.0 million. The data ends in 2018, so it does not include the COVID-19 collapse in travel.
